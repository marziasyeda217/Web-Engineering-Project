MUASHRA CIVIC ENTERPRISE

Har Complaint Ki Awaaz,
Har Sarkari Idaray Ki Zimmedari

Report civic infrastructure issues in your neighborhood using just your voice or text. Muashra converts your complaint into a formal administrative petition, routes it to the exact municipal department, and tracks it until verified resolution.

National Civic Duty & Institutional Accountability Empowering citizens with SLA-tracked digital governance.

Bridging Citizens & Municipal Infrastructure

Designed for effortless civic participation with zero red tape.

🎤

Urdu Voice Transcription

Speak freely in Urdu or Roman Urdu. Built-in speech-to-text turns your voice into structured administrative petitions.

⚡

Precision Department Routing

Automated classification assigns complaints directly to SEPCO, WASA, KW&SB, or SSWMB with verified response SLAs.

🗳️

Community Upvote & Intensify

Neighbors can vote to intensify issues. Critical votes trigger automatic escalation to Deputy Commissioners & Secretaries.

How MUASHRA Works in 4 Simple Steps

1
Speak or Type

Dictate in Urdu or type your grievance in Roman Urdu or English.

2
Live GPS Pinpoint

Click "Detect Live GPS" to automatically link your exact coordinates.

3
AI Formats Petition

Groq AI categorizes the department and drafts formal legal petitions.

4
Officer Resolution

Assigned municipal officers resolve tickets with official verification remarks.

📝

File an Official Public Grievance

Speak in Urdu or type your issue. Category and department are auto-detected.

Ready to record speech

💡 Tip: Click button and speak naturally (e.g. "Humare mohallay mein paani ki line phat gayi hai" or "Bijli ka transformer jal gaya hai").
